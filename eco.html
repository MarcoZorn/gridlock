Gridlock: no fitness function

Nobody scores these cars. Each has a wallet: fares pay in, fuel, wear and crash damage pay out. A car that saves enough has a child, a mutated copy of its brain and its body. A car that goes broke is gone. Passengers tip for speed, freight pays by the load, and there is only so much of either. The generation-based city.

What am I looking at?
  • Every car is coloured by species (or by its current job: blue a passenger, yellow freight, grey nothing to do). The white one is the richest, or the one you clicked.
  • Nobody is scored. A car earns fares, pays for fuel, upkeep and crashes, has a child when it saves enough and is gone when it goes broke.
  • A day is 24 minutes: rush hours at 8:30 and 18:00, freight in business hours, and at night the city empties and the headlights come on.
  • Species over time and the family tree show who is winning; Bodies plots every car's cargo room against its top speed.
  • Edit roads to close a street or build one; Grow adds a district; Pedestrians adds people who have to get across.

The founders are drivers evolved the old way, with a score, in the generation-based city: a world with no fitness function cannot start from random brains, because a car that never finishes a trip never earns, and nothing is ever selected. Half the founders get random bodies. Everything after that is the economy. A day is 24 minutes: watch the morning and evening rush, and the vans that come out in business hours.
