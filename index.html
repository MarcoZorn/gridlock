Gridlock

A city with no traffic laws in the code. Roads, physics and route finding are written by hand; how to drive the route is not. Every car is a NEAT brain that sees a fan of rays, its speed, which way its route goes and how sharp the next turn is, and it has to work out the rest.

What am I looking at?
  • Grey cars are the population; the orange one is the leader (or blue, the car you clicked). Red means just crashed and being towed back to the road.
  • The blue line is the followed car's route from A*, the yellow dot the point on it the car is steering for.
  • The thin lines from it are its five rays: blue where they hit a kerb, orange where they hit another car.
  • Red tail lights mean the brain is braking. Watch how rarely they come on.
  • On the right, the brain is the car's actual network (inputs left, steering and throttle right, evolved neurons between), and the bars under it are everything it feels this instant.
  • Traffic off: every car is a ghost driving the same fares. Traffic on: they share the road and can hit each other.

With traffic off every car is a ghost and all of them drive the same chain of fares, so the scores compare driving and not luck. Turn traffic on once they can drive: then they share the streets, can hit each other, and each one has its own fares. Density is how many are on the road at once; the population drives in waves. The city changes every generation. The original single-track demo is still here.
