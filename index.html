Gridlock

A city with no traffic laws in the code. Roads, physics and route finding are written by hand; how to drive the route is not. Every car is a NEAT brain that sees a fan of rays, its speed, which way its route goes and how sharp the next turn is, and it has to work out the rest.

Controls

With traffic off every car is a ghost and all of them drive the same chain of fares, so the scores compare driving and not luck. Turn traffic on once they can drive: then they share the streets, can hit each other, and each one has its own fares. Density is how many are on the road at once; the population drives in waves. The city changes every generation. The original single-track demo is still here.
